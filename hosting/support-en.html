Frequently Asked Questions

What is Playing Tracker?

Playing Tracker is a music practice companion for students and teachers. Students can log practice sessions and track their progress; teachers can manage classes, assign tasks, and monitor each student's progress.

I can't log in. What should I do?

Make sure you're using the same email you registered with. If you've forgotten your password, tap Forgot password? on the login screen to receive a reset link. If the problem persists, contact us at the email above.

My practice sessions aren't syncing

Playing Tracker requires an internet connection to sync data. Check your connection and open the app again. If the issue continues, please send us your device model and iOS/Android version so we can investigate.

How do I delete my account?

To permanently delete your account and all associated data, send us an email from the address linked to your account and we will process the request within 5 business days.

I found a bug

We appreciate bug reports! Email us with a description of what happened, your device model, and the app version (visible in Settings → About). Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful.
