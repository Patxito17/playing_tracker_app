# Privacy Policy for Playing Tracker
**Version 1.0 · March 2, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
At Playing Tracker, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our educational application.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect the following information when you register and use the application:
- **Account data**: first name, last name, email address, and role (teacher or student).
- **Usage data**: practice sessions (duration, dates), and activity statistics.
- **Class data**: classes you belong to and assigned tasks.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Provide and improve Playing Tracker services.
- Allow teachers to view the progress of their students.
- Sync data across devices via Firebase (Google LLC).
- Send you notifications related to your use of the application.

## 4. Data Sharing
**We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.** We share data only with:
- **Firebase / Google**: For authentication, database, and storage (see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **Your teacher or students**: Within the context of the virtual classroom.

## 5. Data Security
We implement technical and organizational security measures:
- Secure authentication via Firebase Authentication.
- Firestore security rules to control data access.
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).

## 6. Data Retention
We retain your data while you have an active account. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

## 7. User Rights
You have the right to:
- Access the data we hold about you.
- Rectify incorrect data.
- Request deletion of your data.
- Data portability.

To exercise these rights, contact us at [support@playingtracker.app](mailto:support@playingtracker.app).

## 8. Minors
Playing Tracker is designed for educational use. If you are under 14 years old, you need the consent of a parent or guardian to register. Data from minors is handled with special care and confidentiality.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
When we update this policy, we will notify you within the application. Continued use of Playing Tracker after such changes implies acceptance of the new policy.

## 10. Contact
For privacy inquiries:
- Email: [support@playingtracker.app](mailto:support@playingtracker.app)

*This application was developed for educational purposes.*
